Reading this table: Trend is Minervini's 8-point Stage-2 checklist (price/MA stacking, 52-week range, relative strength). Legs is the number of detected contraction pullbacks on the weekly chart. Vol Dry-Up and Tightness are 0–100 sub-scores; RS is an IBD-style percentile rank of momentum across this scan (70+ is Minervini's usual floor). Extension is how far the last close sits above (+) or below (−) the confirmed pivot (the base's high / breakout trigger). Scores peak for setups sitting close to the pivot on either side; stocks already run well past it are marked down as extended, no longer an ideal entry. Stop-Loss is a suggested exit price: just under the low of the most recent contraction when that's tighter, otherwise an 8% hard cap on loss from the last close — hover a value to see which basis applies. This is a heuristic pattern match, not investment advice — always confirm on the chart before acting on a score.
